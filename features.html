Features for your website-to-app project.

WebToApp turns your existing website into an Android or iOS app using WebView technology. Branding, navigation, notifications, advertising, and device integrations depend on what your app and target platforms support. Each one is labelled below.

The approach

A native shell around the website you already run.

WebView displays your website inside the app. Your site remains the source of content. WebToApp settings define the parts of the app around it: its identity, navigation, and the native features you choose, where supported.

  1. 1

    Your website provides the content

    Pages, products, articles, and account areas come from the site you already maintain.

  2. 2

    Project settings shape the app

    Name, icon, splash screen, theme, and navigation are set for each app.

  3. 3

    Native features are added where supported

    Notifications, device access, and analytics need configuration, permissions, and platform support.
