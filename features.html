Feature FAQ

Questions about app features.

Feature support can vary by platform, website, permissions, and project setup. Confirm the capabilities you need before getting started.

Are all features included in every plan?

Not necessarily. Plan limits and feature availability should be confirmed for your chosen platform and project before purchase.

Do device features require user permissions?

Yes. Capabilities such as camera, microphone, and location access depend on platform permissions and the way your website uses them.

Can I add push notifications or sign-in integrations?

Integrations such as Firebase Push Notifications and Google Sign-In are listed as capabilities, but their availability and setup requirements need confirmation for your app.

How does app content stay up to date?

The app displays your website through WebView, so content generally follows website updates. Caching and app behavior can affect when changes appear.

Can I show ads in the app?

AdMob banner and interstitial ads are listed as possible add-ons. Availability, consent requirements, and store policies should be reviewed for your app.
