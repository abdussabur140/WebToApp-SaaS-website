Pricing FAQ

Questions about plans and pricing.

The table is a planning guide. Confirm feature inclusions and exclusions for your selected platform and project before purchase.

Are plan prices monthly?

Yes. The published prices shown are $19/month for Starter, $49/month for Growth, and $99/month for Scale.

Does the platform switch change the price?

The Android and iOS switch lets you view the plans by platform. Pricing and availability still need confirmation for your project.

What does a check mark or cross mean?

A check or cross shows the availability currently listed in the comparison. Confirm all inclusions and exclusions for your selected platform and project before purchase.

What should I confirm before choosing a plan?

Confirm platform availability, app limits, integrations, and build or publishing scope for the plan you need.
