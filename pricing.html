Pricing FAQ

A few things to know.

Quick answers about plans, platforms, and the demo comparison.

Are these feature inclusions confirmed?

No. The check and cross marks are sample content to demonstrate how plan features could be compared. Actual plan inclusions still need confirmation.

Are plan prices monthly?

Yes. The published prices shown are $19/month for Starter, $49/month for Growth, and $99/month for Scale.

Does the platform switch change the price?

The Android and iOS switch is a display demo. It does not indicate different platform pricing or confirmed availability.

What does a check mark or cross mean?

In this demo only, a check means an example feature inclusion and a cross means an example exclusion. They are not final plan details.

What should I confirm before choosing a plan?

Confirm platform availability, app limits, integrations, and build or publishing scope for the plan you need.
